# Their Lives

The Smilovic and Spitz families, one life at a time, by Arthur J. Steingart. Family stories and media are encrypted; the site opens with the family password.

The protected archive includes individual family entries, the family tree, original sources, and a documentary screening room. The films are labeled production studies with original-interview alternatives. Original testimony narrates silent reconstructed action; actors do not deliver the recorded words. Historical research and language review remain in progress.
